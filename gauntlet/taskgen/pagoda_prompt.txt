The mountain village of Kiyomizu-jō hires you to design its centerpiece shrine.
Write a complete build description of a Japanese pagoda temple complex, in
order, as the visitor experiences it. You are not writing code — you are
describing the build itself, precisely and visually.

Required, in this order:
1. The stone foundation and base platform of the complex.
2. A stone stairway or path ascending through the grounds.
3. A vermillion torii gate marking the entrance to the inner shrine.
4. The main pagoda: at least three stacked tiers, each narrower than the one
   below, with flared roof eaves on every tier.
5. The curvature of the roofs — how the eaves sweep and lift at the corners.
6. Lighting: stone lanterns along the path and glowing lanterns hung at the
   topmost tier.
7. The garden: sakura trees, raked gravel, a reflection pond.
8. The backdrop: the mountain face behind the shrine and the water feature
   beside it.
9. An atmospheric closing line: mist, dusk bells, or incense smoke.

Write it as flowing prose, one paragraph per numbered element, in order.
Do not number the paragraphs. Begin directly with the foundation.
